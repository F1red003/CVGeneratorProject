What does a CV consist of? ▼

A CV generally includes the following information:

Essential personal details: First and last name, address, city, email address, phone number(s)...

Optional/additional information: ID photo, gender, date of birth, place of birth, nationality, marital status, driving license, link to a website or your social media profiles (LinkedIn, Facebook).

Academic and/or professional background: Your education (include the date of obtaining a certificate or diploma and its issuance date), your professional experiences (employers, positions, and tasks performed), your skills, language proficiency, courses (training), internships you have completed, extracurricular activities (such as volunteer work), your references, hobbies, and personality traits.

All information must be complete and accurate (pay attention to languages, which you should actually be proficient in).

Tips for Writing the Perfect CV? ▼

In CraftMyCV, you will find advice grouped by themes to help you craft an optimal CV. Here are some general tips:

Only provide relevant information that adds value to the job you are applying for.

Avoid mentioning hobbies or interests that might seem odd or raise questions.

The main information should appear on the first page. Include a brief description of your personal profile.

Use bullet points and numbered lists to improve the clarity of your CV for recruiters.

Always opt for a chronological CV unless otherwise specified in the job offer.

Be concise but convincing when presenting your CV. Mention important information in a clear and concise manner.
